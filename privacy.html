01 Overview

HYPE GENERATOR is a "join to create" voice channel manager: when someone joins a designated channel, the bot creates a personal temporary voice channel for them and gives them controls (lock, trust, block, rename, user limit, kick, invite, and similar) through buttons and forms inside Discord.

To do that, the bot needs to remember a small set of technical identifiers and preferences. It does not read, log, or store the content of your messages.

03 What we don't collect

  • Message content from your server's text channels
  • Voice audio or recordings from voice channels
  • Email addresses, phone numbers, or real names
  • IP addresses or device information
  • Payment information (the bot is not monetized)

04 How we use it

Stored data is used strictly to run the features you interact with: creating and cleaning up temporary channels, applying your lock/trust/block/limit preferences, showing your trusted/blocked list back to you, and enabling the ownership-claim system after prolonged absence. Data is never used for advertising, profiling, or analytics unrelated to these features.

05 Storage & security

Data is stored in a local database file on the hosting infrastructure that runs the bot. Access to that infrastructure is limited to the bot's operator. As with any online service, no method of storage is 100% secure, but reasonable steps are taken to protect the data described above from unauthorized access.

06 Retention & deletion

Active-channel records exist only while your temporary channel exists and are removed automatically once it's deleted (typically when it becomes empty). Saved preferences (your default channel name and user limit) and trusted/blocked lists persist across sessions so they can be reused next time, until you ask for them to be removed or the bot is removed from your server.

You can request deletion of any data tied to your Discord user ID at any time — see Contact below.

07 Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. Data may only be exposed to:

  • Discord, Inc. — as the platform the bot operates on, subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy.
  • The hosting provider running the bot's server process and database, solely as infrastructure.
  • Law enforcement or legal authorities, only if required by applicable law.

08 Server admin access

Note: Server Administrators can use built-in bot commands to export or replace the bot's entire database file for backup or migration purposes. If you administrate a server using this bot, you are responsible for handling any exported data securely and in line with this policy.

09 Your choices & rights

  • Access or delete your data by contacting us with your Discord user ID.
  • Stop use immediately by no longer using the bot's commands, or by having a server admin remove the bot from your server — this deletes that server's associated records.
  • Trusted/blocked lists can be edited or cleared any time via the bot's own panel commands.

10 Children's privacy

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age in your country). This bot does not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age, beyond the standard Discord user ID any bot receives when interacted with.

11 Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the bot's features change. The "Effective" date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the bot after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
